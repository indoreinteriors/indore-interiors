About Us

We Provide Best Affordable Interior Solutions

Indore Interiors is a premier interior solutions and execution firm based in Madhya Pradesh, transforming homes and commercial spaces with creativity, functionality, and elegance.

Founded in 2015 as Homesazz Interiors by Lt. Er. Mohit Garg, the company has evolved into Indore Interiors while maintaining its commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

With a dedicated team of designers, architects, and craftsmen, we specialize in turnkey home interiors, commercial projects, modular kitchens, customized furniture, wardrobes, false ceilings, and complete space transformations tailored to modern lifestyles.

Our focus on thoughtful design, attention to detail, premium execution, and timely delivery has made Indore Interiors a trusted name in the industry.
